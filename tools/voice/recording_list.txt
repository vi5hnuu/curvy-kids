CurvyKids - voice recording list

Format: one file per numbered line, saved at exactly the path after the arrow,
under the assets/voice/ folder of the pack that owns it (see audio/packs/VoicePack.kt):
voice_hi_letters/ or voice_hi_barakhadi/src/main/assets/voice/, else app/src/main/assets/voice/.
Mono, 44.1 kHz, AAC .m4a (or .mp3), 64-96 kbps. Trim the silence at both ends.
Warm, slow, clear voice for young children. Lines not recorded yet use the
phone's built-in voice, so recordings can be added a few at a time.
